# Cornell’s Future of the American University — Brief for WSU Carson College of Business

A static discussion brief for **Carson College of Business** leadership — department chairs, faculty, and the dean’s office.

The page summarizes Cornell University’s *Report of the Committee on the Future of the American University* (September 22, 2026) for a Carson conversation. It is a **discussion brief**. It is not Cornell policy, not a Cornell-internal memo, and not a WSU policy statement. Figures appear only when they are in that discussion brief. None are WSU or Carson statistics.

**GitHub Pages:** [https://chance144.github.io/carson-cornell-brief/](https://chance144.github.io/carson-cornell-brief/)

Pages serves the `docs/` folder on `main`. No custom domain.

## View locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory docs
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

## What’s on the page

- Oral brief for reading aloud
- Why the report matters to a public business school
- Executive takeaways
- Land-grant framing: teaching and learning, research and discovery, extension and engagement, and access and service to Washington
- Disruptors, undergraduate education, graduate and professional programs, research, public impact, and governance
- Roads not taken, Carson discussion questions, and the source citation

## Files
